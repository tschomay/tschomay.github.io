## The problem
What was actually going on — for a non-technical reader, in a sentence or two.
Why was this worth someone's time at all?

## The non-obvious call
What did you almost do differently, and why didn't you? What tradeoff did you
knowingly accept? This is the section that isn't a build log — it's the part
only you could have written, because it's about judgment, not code.

## What happened
Brief. What you built or tried, and the outcome — including if it didn't work
or you'd do it differently next time. Specifics and honesty beat polish here.

## The takeaway
One idea that generalizes past this specific project — something about how you
approach ambiguous problems that a reader could apply to something unrelated.
